# Localized Merge tutorial recordings

160 original MP3s: 40 Heaven/angel and 40 Hell/demon cues in each of Spanish and Brazilian
Portuguese. Copy, voices and settings are in `configs/voice_localization/spanish_portuguese.json`.
The manifest retains exact request provenance, source cue identity, decoded duration and SHA-256.
Approved pilot recordings are copied byte for byte with `reused_from` provenance.

Recordings include main lessons, Auto Collector branches, power help, reminders, completion and
the optional advice to spread eggs across hatchers. They do not add or change gameplay gates.
Original MP3s use the established voice gains at runtime. Upload operation records preserve
asset IDs through Roblox review; upload acceptance does not by itself mean an asset can play.

Use the [full listening page](../tutorial_localized/listen.html) through the local server described
in the [Farm & Fight recording README](../tutorial_localized/README.md).
