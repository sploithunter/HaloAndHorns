# Section 06 — The Impaler Court and Champions’ Stand

Design review, 2026-09-08. **Proposal only; no map, assets, FX, or gameplay changed.** The ambition is a small, beautifully built spectator arena: black iron and warm carved stone, a clear fighting floor, an inhabited grandstand, and a deliberate transition to the green-misted fishing pool behind it. Premium quality here comes first from believable construction, useful seats, and controlled views; additional spectacle follows those foundations.

## Twenty improvements

Effort estimates are focused implementation days after review, excluding asset-service queue time. Dimensions are proposed construction envelopes, to be measured in the native preview. Acceptance criteria describe future tests, not completed tests.

### 01 — Make every seat a useful view

**Observation/benefit:** Image 4 has aligned chair backs; images 2–3 show palisade tips between viewers and low action. The problem is especially the near fighting floor, not proof that all combat is invisible. **Build:** At the existing 32 seat anchors, measure eye rays to a 5×7 target grid at arena floor+2 and +4.5. Trial deck heights 7.5/10/12.5/15 instead of 6/8/10/12, preserving row X and seat count. Stagger occupied columns ±1 stud within existing row bays while retaining the eight aisle seats and 8-stud aisle. **Method:** Native tiers; no bulwark shortening. **Dependencies:** Finalize before stairs, walls, chairs or canopy. **Effort/risk:** 1–2 days; taller rear wall/ramp consequences. **Acceptance:** Every visitor seat sees center and both entrances; at least 80% of torso-height targets clear other chairs/dummies; record remaining near-edge floor occlusion rather than hiding it in camera framing.

### 02 — Construct a clear spectator promenade

**Observation/benefit:** Images 1/5 show ample-looking dark ground but weak circulation definition. **Build:** Two continuous 12-stud end walks beyond Z−28 and Z44, nominal centers Z−35 and Z51, joining the arena-side approach to the pond routes. Where existing paths overlap, replace their footprints with adjoining stone pieces. Keep the strip X152–159 clear along the front; it is only seven studs and must not become a furniture gallery. **Method:** Native stone pavers with one surface elevation and cleaved boundaries. **Dependencies:** Pool designer checks platform access, arena designer keeps south gate clear. **Effort/risk:** 1 day; corner widths need native measurement. **Acceptance:** Two avatars pass at both ends; a player can reach the pond without walking through combat or an occupied seating row.

### 03 — Give the upper tier an alternate ramp approach

**Observation/benefit:** Image 5 reads as a solid platform accessible primarily by stairs. **Build:** Reserve an 8-stud-wide, two-run switchback beyond the north stand end, approximately X160–208/Z−56…−30. Length is driven by approved deck height: rise 11 to a Y15 rear deck needs 88 studs of total ramp at 1:8, plus flat turning landings. Draw this before carving terrain; extend north, not into the pool’s west dock. **Method:** Native ramp collision under stepped stone dressing; 8×8 minimum turn landings. **Dependencies:** 01/02 and pavilion connector review. **Effort/risk:** 1–2 days; proposed envelope may require a longer northern return. **Acceptance:** Walk both directions at 24 without jumping, camera obstruction or entry into combat; retain a full 12-stud pool route outside ramp rails.

### 04 — Make the central stair legible and safe to descend

**Observation/benefit:** Image 4’s near-black step edges merge at grazing view. **Build:** Preserve the 8-stud aisle; rebuild regular 0.5-stud risers for final deck elevations, with a 0.3-stud-wide contrasting stone nosing cut into each tread, rather than laid nearly coplanar over it. Add low recessed amber markers at every second step, outside the foot corridor. **Method:** Native solids and small opaque accent inserts; no per-step lights. **Dependencies:** 01, with seating exit tested. **Effort/risk:** 0.5–1 day; misaligned noses can snag movement. **Acceptance:** All steps read at low graphics and desaturated view; run up/down at 24; no flicker during a low camera sweep or collision bumps.

### 05 — Turn the rear retaining wall into architecture

**Observation/benefit:** Image 5’s long blank black slab is the main view from fishing. **Build:** Dress X199 rear face with seven shallow piers at roughly 12-stud centers, a continuous stone footing, inset 6×4 shield relief bays and a cap under the rail. Keep protrusion ≤1.5 studs; use actual recesses or replacement wall panels, not coplanar decals. If 01 raises the wall, use two stone courses with a horizontal ledge, not stretched texture. **Method:** Existing bone/metal ornament plus Blender modular pier and relief kit if needed. **Dependencies:** 01 and the west dock clearance. **Effort/risk:** 1–2 days; excessive relief eats bank route. **Acceptance:** Fishing-camera screenshot shows a composed façade; narrowest dock-to-wall passage retains agreed clearance with no railing/ornament collisions.

### 06 — Upgrade the chairs without losing real seating

**Observation/benefit:** Image 3 reads as generic reddish chairs, detached from the elaborate bulwark language. **Build:** Keep each 3.2×3 seat cushion and native Seat, surround it with a charcoal iron frame, warm leather inset and shallow horn-like shoulder detail no higher than the existing back. Bevel edges and add two tiny bronze fasteners. Keep ornament inside each chair’s current bay. **Method:** One reusable Blender-authored chair shell, optional ImageGen material study; existing native Seat remains the functional core. **Dependencies:** 01; define seated avatar envelope before mesh. **Effort/risk:** 1–2 days; backs/armrests clipping bodies. **Acceptance:** Sit/jump out of all eight visitor seats, standard and larger avatar tests; exactly 24 disabled preview seats and eight enabled Seats; no visible shell/Seat z-fighting. Native Seat behavior supports real sitting without a custom substitute [S1].

### 07 — Make the placeholder audience look intentionally staged

**Observation/benefit:** Images 3/4 show identical rigid figures in conspicuous same-color rows. **Build:** Keep 24 existing dummies, distribute the existing four metal finishes across columns as small 2–3-person groups, and author four restrained static poses: hands together, forearms resting, one hand raised below head height, slight attentive lean. Preserve head/limb envelopes and reserve every aisle-adjacent empty seat. **Method:** Repose existing static parts; do not install Humanoids or animations. **Dependencies:** 01/06 and sightline checks. **Effort/risk:** 0.5 day; overly expressive poses obscure viewers. **Acceptance:** Still 24 anchored noncolliding spectators, eight clearly available chairs, no limbs through armrests. Future winner-avatar mapping remains metadata only; these are not represented as actual ranked users.

### 08 — Give visitors clear physical seat and row markers

**Observation/benefit:** Empty chairs in image 4 are easy to mistake for decoration. **Build:** Small 1.2×0.6 bronze seat plates on the eight visitor chair fronts, with an embossed chair symbol; row markers A–D in 0.8-stud letters at aisle ends. Put a matching 3×2 physical “WATCH” direction plaque at the stair foot, outside the aisle. **Method:** Existing extruded glyph workflow and simple Blender/native relief, not floating BillboardGui. **Dependencies:** 06, localization/copy review later. **Effort/risk:** 0.5 day; excessive microtext becomes clutter. **Acceptance:** Seat function recognizable from 12 studs by symbol; no nameplates imply current leaderboard winners; labels do not cover characters or require color discrimination.

### 09 — Give the combat floor a crafted identity

**Observation/benefit:** Image 1 shows a nearly empty dark rectangle with only two cover blocks. **Build:** Replace the floor finish with broad 12–16-stud irregular charcoal stone fields and a 20-stud central engraved halo/horn medallion at (120,4,8). Use restrained weathered brass/stone, not bright combat telegraphs. Keep the exact floor envelope and level; seams are physical boundaries or baked texture detail with no layered planar sheets. **Method:** Blender tessellated floor kit using shared PBR maps or native non-overlapping slabs; albedo/normal/roughness variation supplies fine wear [S2]. **Dependencies:** Snapshot floor/cover/routes before replacement. **Effort/risk:** 1–2 days; busy texture reduces pet/enemy readability. **Acceptance:** No flicker from all five review cameras; body silhouettes remain distinct at low graphics; all route markers and 64×102 bounds unchanged.

### 10 — Explain how the bulwarks emerge

### 11 — Replace anonymous low cover with believable broken monuments

### 12 — Finish the encounter pavilion as a ceremonial marshal’s ruin

### 14 — Give the stand a distinctive silhouette without roofing over the action

### 16 — Plant the architecture into the landscape

## Top five priorities and build order

Priority order: **01 sightlines; 02 promenade; 04 stair clarity; 05 rear façade; 09 crafted floor.** Those address the largest visible and functional weaknesses before adding spectacle. Item 03 is a dependent access requirement once the final tiers are fixed, even though it is not a separate decorative priority.

**Phase A — Survey and dimensional lock (1–2 days):** Retain the corrected whole-map overview; capture empty and occupied versions of all five arena views, plus all eight visitor-seat cameras and both gate thresholds. Record actual bulwark tops, avatar eye heights, west dock edges, tier elevations and paving seams. Trial 01 with temporary native solids; resolve 02/03 with the pool/pavilion designers. Deliver one dimensioned plan/section and a ray-visibility matrix. Do not start custom assets while access or height remains unsettled.

**Phase B — Walkable architectural pass (2–3 days):** Build 02/03/04/09/10/11/13 using source-owned config dimensions and Edit companions. Preserve geometry backups and containment attributes. Run the same player route at speed 24: south gathering → west gateway → south gateway → stands → upper-tier ramp → both end walks → west fishing station. Check close/grazing camera seams on both sides of every new junction. No floor overlaps to camouflage with trim.

**Phase C — Modular art and seating (3–5 days):** Complete shared stone/iron chair/rail kit, then 05/06/07/08/12/14/15/16/19/20. One successful chair and one wall bay become production templates before repeating them. Capture seat views again after art, because the mesh shell—not just collision—is what blocks players’ view. Compare high/low graphics and day/Hell approach look. Stop adding ornament if the Siege gate loses landmark priority.

**Phase D — Restrained FX and final review (1–2 days):** Add 17; evaluate 18 as a static specimen and separately priced future capability. Compare arena+pool mist together. Profile loaded section, not isolated meshes. Deliver side-by-side old/new shots, native walk-through, seat-count audit, dependency list, and any unresolved sightline/clearance compromises. Estimates overlap across kit reuse; these are planning ranges, not a delivery promise.

## Asset production briefs

**Stone-and-iron kit:** Six reusable pieces: straight façade bay, pier, cap, rail, end post, chair shell. Charcoal rough stone, warm oxidized iron, small old-bronze accents, broad readable bevels; no photo-real grunge or giant ornamental spikes. Chair shell target ≤1,200 triangles; repeated façade/rail pieces ≤800 each; these are initial budgets, not measured assets. Shared 1024 maps where practical. Functional seat/collision/rail volumes stay native. Keep texture coordinates and material slots consistent across the kit.

**Marshal ruin and standards:** Reuse existing Heaven/Hell banners, skull lantern and native palisade vocabulary first. Optional ImageGen board: front/side/isometric, neutral background, standard 5.5-stud avatar, compact broken lintel, two short pennants, separated stone/metal parts, no floor, no lettering baked into mesh. Meshy generates only missing decorative forms. Blender must fix scale, topology, UV seams, grounding, LOD candidates and pivots; exact letterforms remain native/Blender typography. Produce Assets source/export/native artifacts and config references before Roblox upload, with group permissions and texture visibility verified.

**Champion reliefs:** Two panels, each 5×7×≤0.6 studs, chunky pet/egg silhouettes from actual roster references, 3–4 depth levels, clear negative space; target ≤1,500 triangles each. Reuse a shared material set. ImageGen may supply composition references; generated fake words or unfamiliar animals are rejected. Reliefs are read from oblique fishing-bank cameras, not only a front-on asset render.

**Optional FX art:** One shared ember sprite and one crystal bloom preset, not a suite of unrelated effects. Use existing textures before new generation. All new asset IDs, palette values, dimensions, rates and distance budgets belong in configs or config-named art companions; none hardcoded into gameplay services.

## Interfaces, accessibility and performance acceptance

- **Hell fishing:** Share the north/south end-route plan and rear wall projection with its owner. Preserve eight stations, local green mist, global water settings, and a 12-stud bank circulation objective. At the western station, measure the actual stand–dock gap; route behind/around the dock deliberately if a full 12 studs cannot fit, rather than silently reducing it. No new trees, buttresses or ramps in the gap by assumption.
- **Central hub and Siege:** West entrance remains at the established stair connection; maintain the 14-stud clear span and legible approach. New standards stay subordinate to Siege gate/title. Do not rotate the gate or repaint shared paths as part of this section.
- **Pavilion/Bragg:** Northern stand access must not cross encounter spawn space or pinch the Bragg connector. Champion symbols may repeat Bragg motifs, but no new stats, rankings or real-user displays are introduced.
- **Future combat contract:** Preserve visual bulwark attributes, current bounds and route markers. Do not activate collision on the decorative perimeter as a substitute for later combatant-only containment; the wider hub’s invisible safety boundary is a separate invariant. Later patrol/alliance recruitment and leash ranges require explicit compact-arena review.
- **Access:** Eight visitor Seats remain reachable and usable. Test seated exit, free camera, small/large avatar silhouettes, controller navigation and 24-stud/sec movement. Do not force a cinematic or spectator camera. Distinguish routes/steps by value and shape as well as hue; avoid flashes and always provide reduced-FX alternatives for future celebration.
- **Initial art budgets:** Reconcile these local targets with the seven other section plans before implementation; the map already extends to |X|300 and this proposal does not widen it further. Aim for ≤45,000 additional decorative triangles across this section, shared texture families, no more than four small ambient emitters and two nearby shadowless lights in the normal arena view. Proposed numbers are budgets to validate, not universal Roblox limits. Existing dummies remain static/no Humanoids; avoid per-dummy updates. Keep mesh décor noncolliding and use simple collision proxies.
- **Combined load:** Test the stand view with all 24 dummies, eight visitor avatars and Hell pool mist visible, then a synthetic later-combat load separately. Record frame-time, memory, transparent overdraw and device before/after. Target no more than a 10% idle-section frame-time regression on the agreed baseline device; reduce particles, light count and ornamental detail before reducing functional seating. Official guidance emphasizes profiling and asset reuse, not triangle counts alone [S4].
- **Visual sign-off:** Repeat images 1–5 from identical cameras, all eight occupied visitor views, a rear wall shot from the west fishing dock, two gate approaches and low grazing floor sweeps. Clear seams, usable seating, visible action and a readable pond route are release conditions for this art pass.
