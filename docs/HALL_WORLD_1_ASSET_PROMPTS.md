# Hall of Worlds — World 1 Asset Prompts

These prompts are intentionally constrained for image-to-mesh conversion and reliable gold
variants. Generate the normal model first. Use the gold-variant prompt at the end with the approved
normal render as the image reference.

Use this after approving a normal render:

> Using the approved base render as the exact shape reference, create the identical character or
> egg with the same pose, proportions, silhouette, attachments, and facial features. Convert the
> primary materials to polished faceted gold with amber and cream contrast while preserving the
> eyes and important markings. Do not alter geometry or add effects. Model ready, texture baked,
> solid opaque attached geometry, on a plain white background, no shadows or ground clutter, no
> text, no smoke, no wisps, no transparency, no floating parts, no disconnected accessories, and
> no extremely thin pieces.
