# Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese voice pilot

2026-09-08 · 12 samples · Listening review only

Each reel plays **welcome → instruction → reminder**, with a one-second pause between clips. The supplied angel and demon voice IDs and multilingual-v2 settings are unchanged. Original MP3s remain untouched; listening reels are normalized to the same loudness for a fair accent/tone comparison. This does not change in-game volume settings.

Listen for natural pronunciation, a warm angel, a lightly mocking demon, and understandable game terms—especially **Waycoins**, **incubadoras**, and the Earth Egg name. Translations are character-led adaptations; fluent-speaker review remains the approval step before a full batch.

## Spanish — Angel

[Play the three-sample reel](../assets/audio/voices/localization_pilot/reels/es_angel.mp3)

### Welcome · ES_A01

Ahí estás, pequeña luz. Vamos a elegir a tu primer compañero. Un conejito, un osito, un perrito o un gatito... ¿quién te acompañará en esta aventura?

**English meaning:** There you are, little light. Let’s choose your first companion. A bunny, a bear, a doggy, or a kitty... who will join you on this adventure?

[Original sample](../assets/audio/voices/localization_pilot/es/angel/ES_A01.mp3)

### Instruction · ES_A02

Tus guardianes están listos. Prueba repartir tu defensa entre más incubadoras. Dos escuadrones pequeños pueden ayudarte más que un solo huevo poderoso. Tú decides.

**English meaning:** Your guardians are ready. Try spreading your defense across more hatchers. Two small squads can help more than one powerful egg. You decide.

[Original sample](../assets/audio/voices/localization_pilot/es/angel/ES_A02.mp3)

### Reminder · ES_A03

Tu próximo amiguito te está esperando. Sigue el camino brillante hasta el Huevo de Tierra y abre uno. Yo estaré aquí.

**English meaning:** Your next little friend is waiting. Follow the glowing path to the Earth Egg and open one. I’ll be right here.

[Original sample](../assets/audio/voices/localization_pilot/es/angel/ES_A03.mp3)
